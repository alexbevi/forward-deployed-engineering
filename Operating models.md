# Operating models

Compare how a function is intended to work before adopting its practices. These descriptions are source-grounded and provisional; they are not proven templates or mutually exclusive categories.

[Operating models](Bases/Operating%20models.md)

[Product-oriented FDE](Operating%20models/Product-oriented%20FDE.md) and [Customer-owned delivery](Operating%20models/Customer-owned%20delivery.md) differ in their central deliverable. [Outcome-scoped missions](Operating%20models/Outcome-scoped%20missions.md) describes engagement scope. [Partner production delivery](Operating%20models/Partner%20production%20delivery.md) describes technical readiness and leaves many operating decisions unknown. Do not fill those gaps using another company's model.

[Atlassian co-engineering](Operating%20models/Atlassian%20co-engineering.md) supplies a concrete phased model combining product and receiving-team capability aims.

[Ground-prove-expand-transfer delivery](Operating%20models/Ground-prove-expand-transfer%20delivery.md) extracts CTERA's progression from operating evidence, through a first production use case and expansion, to a receiving team able to add the next use case. It adds a repeatability and transition test, but remains a vendor-reported model rather than a proven general sequence.

[Cresta's account](Articles/2026-10-06%20-%20Jove%20Zhong%20-%20Building%20a%20world-class%20FDE%20team.md) strengthens [Product-oriented FDE](Operating%20models/Product-oriented%20FDE.md) with an engineering reporting line, direct product contributions, and shared-platform reuse. It does not describe customer handover or prove that availability to later deployments produces value.

When comparing, ask who sets priorities, who can approve a change, who maintains the result, and which evidence closes the engagement. Return to [FDE](FDE.md).
