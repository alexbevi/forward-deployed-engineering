# Operating models

Compare how a function is intended to work before adopting its practices. These descriptions are source-grounded and provisional; they are not proven templates or mutually exclusive categories.

![[Bases/Operating models.base]]

[[Operating models/Product-oriented FDE]] and [[Operating models/Customer-owned delivery]] differ in their central deliverable. [[Operating models/Outcome-scoped missions]] describes engagement scope. [[Operating models/Partner production delivery]] describes technical readiness and leaves many operating decisions unknown. Do not fill those gaps using another company's model.

[[Operating models/Atlassian co-engineering]] supplies a concrete phased model combining product and receiving-team capability aims.

[[Operating models/Ground-prove-expand-transfer delivery]] extracts CTERA's progression from operating evidence, through a first production use case and expansion, to a receiving team able to add the next use case. It adds a repeatability and transition test, but remains a vendor-reported model rather than a proven general sequence.

[[Articles/2026-10-06 - Jove Zhong - Building a world-class FDE team|Cresta's account]] strengthens [[Operating models/Product-oriented FDE]] with an engineering reporting line, direct product contributions, and shared-platform reuse. It does not describe customer handover or prove that availability to later deployments produces value.

When comparing, ask who sets priorities, who can approve a change, who maintains the result, and which evidence closes the engagement. Return to [[FDE]].
