# Resources

Tools, learning material, and documentation discovered through the catalogue. The status describes our engagement with the resource, not its quality.

[FDEOps](Resources/FDEOps.md) is a reviewed candidate for task guidance and engagement memory with AI coding agents. Its note records the inspected revision, setup requirements, evidence limits and a proposed trial.

[Resources](Bases/Resources.md)

`mentioned` means a reviewed source identifies it; `reviewed` means its own material was inspected; `used` means a recorded local use exists. A directly supplied tool can be catalogued from its own documentation without an article note. Record availability and verification dates where meaningful. A mentioned tool is a lead, not an endorsement, and a reviewed programme is not a demonstrated learning outcome.

Reusable technical methods belong in [Approaches](Approaches.md); procedures and small tips belong in [Playbooks](Playbooks.md). Give a resource its own note when it has a useful purpose, constraints, or evidence worth maintaining.
