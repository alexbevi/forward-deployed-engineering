# What is a Forward Deployed Product Manager, anyway?

[Read the article](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/what-is-a-forward-deployed-product-manager-anyway/).

## Adjacent function

Clark describes a vendor-employed FDPM embedded with a customer, pairing account-level discovery and value delivery with an FDE's technical implementation. A core-platform PM connects field evidence to broader product decisions. Her role questions test roadmap authority and the engineering capacity backing commitments.

Catalogued at the user's request as a peripheral function relevant to FDE collaboration. Compare [Product-oriented FDE](../Operating%20models/Product-oriented%20FDE.md) and [Scope an engagement](../Playbooks/Scope%20an%20engagement.md) for product contribution and decision rights. This is an adjacent role account, not a new FDE operating model.

## Evidence and interpretation

The opening travel-retailer incident is retrospective experience; the later redesign is explicitly imagined. Hiring figures come from The Bench across forward-deployed titles, not FDPM-only counts. The role contrasts are editorial generalisations, not universal ownership rules.

Read alongside [McKinsey's disagreement about consulting boundaries](2026-10-16%20-%20McKinsey%20-%20What%20is%20a%20forward-deployed%20engineer.md) and [Cresta's combined business and technical judgment](2026-10-06%20-%20Jove%20Zhong%20-%20Building%20a%20world-class%20FDE%20team.md). Our synthesis retains discovery and product judgment within FDE practice; this overview does not justify assigning them exclusively to a separate PM.

`FDE.md` stays unchanged because its mandate and decision-authority guidance already accommodates this comparison. A useful follow-up would be an actual engagement showing how the product counterpart secured core-team capacity and resolved competing account priorities.
