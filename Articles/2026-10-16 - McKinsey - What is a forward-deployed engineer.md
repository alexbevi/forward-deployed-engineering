# What is a forward-deployed engineer?

[Read the McKinsey explainer](https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/mckinsey-explainers/what-is-a-forward-deployed-engineer).

## Contribution and reported practice

McKinsey's edited interview connects software delivery with workflow change. Harrysson and Hürtgen distinguish FDE implementation from advisory consulting; Robson sees convergence.

Harrysson recommends watching users' decisions and data in real work. Hürtgen describes demonstrations and iteration.

Robson reports an industrial supply-chain tool working in roughly 1.5 days. The account does not establish production readiness or a comparable baseline.

## Limits and interpretation

The speakers and cases share company provenance. This is practitioner testimony, not independent evaluation. Maintenance, decision rights and handover remain unspecified.

For comparison, [[Operating models/Product-oriented FDE]] and [[Operating models/Customer-owned delivery]] ask what the engagement leaves behind. Neither follows from calling a practitioner an FDE or consultant. This is our synthesis across sources, not a McKinsey operating model.

The existing [[Playbooks/Scope an engagement]] now makes task observation explicit. That adaptation remains proposed, with no local results. A useful follow-up would compare the observed workflow with the engagement brief to find decisions or dependencies an interview missed.
