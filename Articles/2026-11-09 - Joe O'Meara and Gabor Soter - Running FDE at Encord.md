# Running FDE at Encord

[Read the interview](https://www.forwarddeployed.engineering/p/issue-2-lessons-from-running-the-forward-deployed-engineering-function-at-a-high-growth-startup-just).

Sharps describes qualified customer missions whose pods own technical debt and may defer core product scalability concerns. Strategists map stakeholder workflows; engineers implement changes.

Scoping starts with a capability and dependencies, then a rough timeline and early proof points. Shared briefings and occasional refinement time connect engagements to core product work.

This is a company-specific account. Timing tolerances, hiring tests and AI judgments are practitioner heuristics, not validated thresholds. The workflow adoption example is illustrative. No customer handover, debt retirement, completed productisation or comparative outcomes are demonstrated.
