# Turning intelligence into lasting value

[Read the article](https://berme.io/2026/09/21/how-forward-deployed-engineering-turns-intelligence-into-lasting-value).

## Summary

Bermejo distinguishes agent correctness, operational improvement, and reusable product learning. His hypothetical invoice-dispute workflow measures total effort, including review and correction. Improvements must be tested with other customers before assuming reuse. Lasting value requires another team to operate and change the system; continuing external support can be acceptable when dependencies are understood and supportable.

## Perspective and limits

This is a proposed model with a hypothetical example, not measured deployment results. Investment announcements cited in the essay do not demonstrate outcomes.
