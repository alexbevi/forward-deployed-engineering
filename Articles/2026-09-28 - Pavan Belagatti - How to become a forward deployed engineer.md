# How to become a forward deployed engineer

[Read the article on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-become-forward-deployed-engineer-fde-hottest-tech-belagatti-hrq7c/).

Belagatti uses a fictional request for an incident investigation agent to show how the work changes as a deployment progresses. Agree with the SREs on the investigation bottleneck and a time target. Use the customer's network constraints to design the integration, then prototype one incident workflow. Real deployment adds permissions, service ownership mappings, and visible failures when a connector drops out. The last decision is which recurring needs to bring back to product engineering.

His skills roadmap pairs software and AI engineering with discovery, integration, production reliability, and responsibility for the outcome after launch. [FDE Mission](../Resources/FDE%20Mission.md) turns the same example into a multiple-choice exercise for discussing those decisions.

## Evidence and limits

The 45-to-8-minute result and perfect score belong to the fictional scenario. The accompanying Port dashboard uses sample figures and the article promotes the product. The article provides no production measurements or evaluation of the roadmap as a learning programme.
