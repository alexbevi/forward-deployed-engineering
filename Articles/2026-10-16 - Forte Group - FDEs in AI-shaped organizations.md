# FDEs in AI-shaped organizations

[Read the Forte article](https://www.fortegrp.com/insights/what-is-a-forward-deployed-engineer-and-why-ai-shaped-organizations-need-one).

## Contribution

Forte proposes embedding engineers in business functions to own problems through production. It compares internal, partner and hybrid staffing, and warns that small teams need continuity planning and clear technical ownership. Its comparison with conventional software engineering is a vendor framing, not an established boundary between roles.

## Reported case and shared provenance

The linked [healthcare case study](https://www.fortegrp.com/case-studies/how-a-5-person-engineering-team-scaled-operations-across-47-healthcare-centers-with-ai), visibly dated March 26, 2026, reports five engineers serving an unnamed provider's 47 centers. Prescription validation reportedly fell from about 20 to 5 minutes, while existing centers absorbed referral growth with flat staffing.

Both pages are Forte's account of the same engagement. Neither provides a measurement period, sample, quality evaluation, full labor accounting, or comparison isolating the role's effect. The case does not document an FDE mandate or decision rights. It supports a reported workflow improvement, not proof that FDE staffing caused it.

## Check against the cited analyst source

[Gartner's October 2 article](https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/ai-shaped-organization) confirms that 74% of conference-poll respondents expected smaller teams and 2% expected larger ones. These are expectations, not observed staffing changes; the public article supplies no poll sample size or recruitment details. Gartner recommends pairing technical and domain expertise, and platform teams for maintenance and retirement. Its argument starts with value drivers rather than projected headcount.

## Interpretation and practical use

Staffing source is a separate choice from mandate, decision authority, and ownership. Internal embedding does not by itself establish a product-feedback loop or receiving-team independence. Compare [[Operating models/Product-oriented FDE]] and [[Operating models/Customer-owned delivery]] without treating Forte's staffing options as equivalent engagement models.

The existing [[Playbooks/Scope an engagement]] and [[Playbooks/Evaluate outcomes and reuse]] cover the relevant decisions. A useful follow-up would obtain the validation-time measurement boundary and recurring human effort before using this case for capacity planning. This is a proposed evidence check, with no local results.
