# Implementation to adoption handoff

[Read the article](https://www.quivly.ai/blog/fde-implementation-to-adoption-handoff).

Jain proposes gradual transfer to customer success, shared readiness criteria, a technical/account/success-plan package and bounded hypercare. Technical and usage signals inform exit; incident ownership and communication are agreed before go-live.

This is commercial guidance, not an evaluated intervention. Claimed 3x account coverage and 50% pre-emptive issue resolution lack methods. Cross-account error patterns are triage clues, not established causes. CS-led cutover does not itself establish production-change authority.

[[Playbooks/Prove a handover]] adds a proposed adoption checkpoint. [[Operating models/Customer-owned delivery]] distinguishes account stewardship from operational independence. `FDE.md` stays unchanged because it already separates launch, usefulness and sustainable ownership.
