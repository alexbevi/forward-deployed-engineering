# What FDE means at OpenAI

[Read the article](https://openai.com/index/forward-deployed-engineering-means/).

Jarvis describes two routes from field work: productize deployments supported by current models, or define capability gaps with customers and Research. Semiconductor workflows reportedly moved onto common Codex skills/plugins, removing roughly 90% of custom code. A voice engagement reportedly improved customer-specific evaluations from about 60% to over 90% across six months.

These are vendor-reported results with insufficient measurement detail for independent assessment. Repeatability across teams does not establish transfer across unrelated customers or customer-independent handover. Companion customer stories share provenance and were not independently reviewed.
