# The rise of the forward deployed engineer

[Read the article](https://vinoo.io/writing/2026-09-12-the-rise-of-the-forward-deployed-engineer/). The page identifies [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/forward-deployed-engineer-best-practices) as its original publication and canonical URL. The date above belongs to the supplied page.

## Summary

Ganesh argues that FDEs should turn customer learning into reusable product capabilities. Observing work reveals constraints that interviews miss. He describes a blocked Parquet migration resolved by restoring an analyst's ability to inspect data, and a temporary retention script that became a maintenance burden. At Kepler, provenance exposes misunderstood business definitions and recurring platform limitations.

## Perspective and limits

This is a practitioner argument informed by Palantir, Citadel, and Kepler. Its strict product-oriented definition needs comparison with other operating models; the anecdotes do not establish universal results.
