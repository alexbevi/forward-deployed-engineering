# Three roles in one engineer

[Read the article](https://brival.co/insights/2026/11/three-roles-one-engineer).

Kupczyk describes a part-time FDE with over 15 years' experience leading three full-time developers, with specialists and QA retained. The lead combines product ownership, design and architecture around rapid prototype reviews with the product manager.

Brival estimates at least five times the scope for roughly 1.5 times the hours across two projects for one client, three years apart. Scope lacks a reproducible measure; the work differs. This uncontrolled comparison cannot isolate AI's contribution. Claimed test coverage does not establish correctness. Product feedback into a shared vendor product and customer-independent handover are not demonstrated.
