# What is an FDE?

[Read the article](https://apeg.dev/forward-deployed-engineer/).

## Summary

Prasath defines FDE work through production accountability inside a customer's systems and rules, ending in usable handover. In his video example, a local reviewer caught footage assigned to the wrong neighbourhood. Replacing proximity-based grouping with geographical boundaries addressed the cause. He recommends establishing decision boundaries, involving knowledgeable reviewers early, and checking that data and a future system owner are available.

## Perspective and limits

This is the overview of the author's commercial engagement model. Its case reports overlap with the rest of his series, so they are not independent corroboration. Its categorical distinctions from consulting and vendor engineering are the author's framing. The asserted search-interest growth is not substantiated here.
