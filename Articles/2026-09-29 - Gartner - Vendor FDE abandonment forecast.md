# Vendor FDE abandonment forecast

[Read the release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-09-29-gartner-predicts-70-percent-of-enterprises-will-abandon-agentic-ai-built-by-vendor-forward-deployed-engineering-by-2028).

## Summary

Gartner forecasts that by 2028, 70% of enterprises will abandon vendor-FDE-built agentic AI because of costs and inability to evolve it independently. It also predicts fewer than 20% of engagements will improve the vendor's core product through 2028.

Its advice spans contracting, joint delivery, and exit: establish ownership and knowledge transfer early, validate business outcomes iteratively, and demonstrate customer independence.

## Perspective and limits

These are forecasts, not measured failure rates. The release gives no methodology; the linked client playbook was not reviewed. Full release text was accessible, but canonical metadata was not verified.
