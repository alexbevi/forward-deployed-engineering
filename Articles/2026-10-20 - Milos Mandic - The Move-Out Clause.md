# The move-out clause

[Read the article](https://www.fdehub.org/p/the-move-out-clause).

Mandic argues that handover should let process owners inspect and change business rules without vendor engineers. Continued platform use can be compatible with that independence. He distinguishes ownership of code and prompts from operational learning engineers carry into later engagements.

His evidence combines reported consulting experience, an explicitly unverified estimate about FDE teams, and reused [[Articles/2026-09-29 - Gartner - Vendor FDE abandonment forecast|Gartner forecasts]]. Their apparent agreement does not independently establish failure rates. The procurement guide he discusses is not linked and was not reviewed.

Our synthesis retains technical ownership and recovery checks alongside rule editing. An editable interface alone does not demonstrate safe maintenance or a completed handover.
