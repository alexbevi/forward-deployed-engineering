# FDE engagements for owners

[Read the article](https://apeg.dev/forward-deployed-engineer/for-owners/).

## Summary

The owner supplies problem definition, access, a knowledgeable reviewer, and decisions about scope, spending, and production changes. The engineer handles delivery between those decisions. Weekly usable outputs and scheduled measurements give the owner evidence for continuing, changing direction, or stopping. Handover includes shipped versions, exclusions, named activation steps, acceptance checks, and customer control of accounts and keys.

## Perspective and limits

This is the owner's version of the author's service model, not independent evidence of its effectiveness. Promised time commitments and schedules reflect his engagements. Existing decisions can be reopened by the owner; documenting them does not make them permanently correct.
