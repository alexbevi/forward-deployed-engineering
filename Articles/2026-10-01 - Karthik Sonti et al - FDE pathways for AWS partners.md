# FDE pathways for AWS partners

[Read the article](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/new-fde-pathways-for-aws-partners-get-ready-for-production-agentic-ai-delivery/).

## Summary

AWS introduces three complementary capability pathways for production agentic AI:

| Pathway | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | Trusted data and business context. |
| Orchestrate | Reliable workflows, identity, and recovery. |
| Prove | Evaluation, observability, security, and human control. |

These are skill areas, not fixed jobs. FDE Validated and FDE Advanced recognise applied proficiency through certification prerequisites and practical validation.

## Perspective and limits

This is an AWS programme announcement, not independent evidence of delivery outcomes. Current credential requirements belong to the linked Skill Builder programme.
