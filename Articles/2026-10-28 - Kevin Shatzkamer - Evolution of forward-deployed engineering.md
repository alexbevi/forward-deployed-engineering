# FDE as a delivery capability

[Read the article](https://commandline.microsoft.com/thinking-about-the-evolution-of-forward-deployed-engineers-over-the-last-24-months/).

Shatzkamer describes FDE as an engineering capability delivered by multidisciplinary pods. He retains product feedback while extending responsibility through workflow redesign, production operation and customer capability transfer. He argues that better coding tools shift the constraint toward organisational ability to turn deployments into reusable capability.

His proposed transfer conditions combine co-engineering, training alongside final sprints, and a named customer owner with support and operating processes.

This is a Microsoft executive's account, without comparative measurements or demonstrated handovers. Its staged chronology is an interpretation, not an established industry history.
