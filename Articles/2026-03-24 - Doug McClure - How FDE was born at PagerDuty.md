# How FDE was born at PagerDuty

[Read the article](https://www.pagerduty.com/eng/from-embedded-to-everywhere-how-forward-deployed-engineering-was-born-at-pagerduty/).

## Summary

McClure traces PagerDuty's services work through a 2019 embedded engagement to product-oriented FDE. Bespoke integrations repeatedly recreated unsupported solutions. Moving into Product Development required learning feature-team standards and delivery practices. Contribution zones distinguish independent work, collaboration, and core-engineering ownership; field signals feed assessment, execution, and shared learning.

## Perspective and limits

This is an internal retrospective, not an independently evaluated comparison. Reported speed lacks comparative measurements.
