# Your engineering strategy has a new failure mode

[Read Robert Kimani's article](https://leaddev.com/leadership/your-engineering-strategy-has-a-new-failure-mode), published by LeadDev on October 12, 2026.

## Contribution and evidence

Kimani argues that AI can move delivery constraints into review, integration and operations. He proposes tracking investment assumptions through observable signals, accountable owners and reassessment triggers, with commitments proportionate to evidence and reversibility.

The opening scenario is hypothetical. Cited surveys and forecasts are supporting references, not an evaluation of the proposed mechanism. Their underlying methods were not reviewed; no local outcomes are recorded.
