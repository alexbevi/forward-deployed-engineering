# How an FDE engagement works

[Read the article](https://apeg.dev/forward-deployed-engineer/how-it-runs/).

## Summary

Prasath describes founder-led delivery with written decision boundaries, weekly usable increments, automated quality gates, service stand-ins, and explicit handover. A small first feature exercises the delivery pipeline. Handoffs name shipped commits, exclusions, activation owners, and checkable acceptance criteria.

His company-specific definition differs from Ganesh's requirement for upstream product improvement.

## Perspective and limits

Self-reported engagement records support a commercial offering; they were not independently inspected. Canonical metadata remains unverified.
