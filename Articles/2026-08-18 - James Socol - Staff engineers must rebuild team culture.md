# Staff+ engineers must rebuild team culture

[Read the article](https://leaddev.com/leadership/staff-engineers-must-rebuild-team-culture).

## Summary

Socol argues that AI-enabled parallel delivery can turn a team into a collection of independently shipping engineers. He uses shared goals, interdependent work, and stable membership as markers of a team, then proposes that Staff+ engineers deliberately reinforce norms through recognition, stories, living team charters, visible reasoning, review, and collaborative planning.

## Perspective and limits

The article combines reported experience with a practitioner argument. It refers broadly to team research but does not present primary studies or measured evidence that AI caused the described change, nor does it evaluate the proposed countermeasures. Independence can remove waste as well as shared learning; the relevant question is which interdependencies improve outcomes rather than whether all friction should be restored.
