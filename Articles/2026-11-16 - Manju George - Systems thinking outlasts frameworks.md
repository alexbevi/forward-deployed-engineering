# Systems thinking outlasts frameworks

[Read the article](https://leaddev.com/technical-direction/systems-thinking-outlasts-frameworks).

George argues that framework expertise needs system context. She recommends following a user workflow through dependencies, failure paths and business consequences. Her Angular library account reports adoption by more than a dozen internal applications. The reusable capability depended on boundaries, extension points, versioning and ownership.

This is practitioner experience, without comparison data, adoption artifacts or quantified maintenance costs. Internal adoption does not establish reuse across customers. The AI section offers review questions, not measured agent results.
