# Are Harnesses Worth the Cost?

[Read the article](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/are-harnesses-worth-the-cost.html). The author is Naresh Jain; Martin Fowler is the publisher.

Jain reports 10 attempts per condition and model on one Java BFF, accepted only after 178 external checks. Development feedback used contracts, examples and mocks; final evaluation was separate.

| Reported model | Accepted with harness | Accepted without harness | Estimated cost with / without |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GPT-6 Sol | 10/10 | 10/10 | $2.679 / $0.406 |
| GPT-6 Luna | 10/10 | 3/10 | $0.273 / $0.086 |

These are author-reported API-equivalent estimates, not billed costs. Harness construction, maintenance and repair effort are excluded. The detailed brief, low reasoning effort and single service limit transfer; guidance and feedback changed together, so their effects are confounded. Final-gate-only verification is a proposed condition, not a tested cheaper workflow. The author founded Specmatic, used in the harness.
