# Elephants, Goldfish and software engineering

[Read Dave Rensin's essay](https://drensin.medium.com/elephants-goldfish-and-the-new-golden-age-of-software-engineering-c33641a48874), published April 27, 2026, with an October 21 plugin announcement.

## Contribution and evidence

Rensin proposes developing the problem, acceptance criteria and design with a context-rich agent, then testing the document through fresh sessions for comprehension, criticism and implementation readiness. Humans retain design judgment and approval. Durable specifications support resumption and onboarding.

This is a Google practitioner's experience account. Adoption and productivity claims lack a controlled comparison, reproducible task sample or complete cost accounting. Predictions about disappearing code and management skills remain the author's outlook. The essay, [project site and repository](../Resources/Elephant-Goldfish%20Model.md) share provenance, not independent corroboration.
