# Staff engineers shift from solving problems to shaping systems

[Read the article](https://leaddev.com/career-development/staff-engineers-shift-from-solving-problems-to-shaping-systems).

## Summary

Selway describes four moves in Staff-level work: identify the gap between the visible question and the problem worth solving; choose a system-level answer rather than automatically making the smallest change; build shared belief through reviews, prototypes, validation, and challenge; and spread the reasoning so improvement continues without the original engineer. Her example replaces warehouse heuristics for customer handling time with information generated by the product at source.

## Perspective and limits

This is a practitioner's reflection on one project and conversations with other senior engineers. It does not report comparative delivery data, adoption measures, or a systematic study. The claim that the right solution is rarely the smallest change should therefore be read as a prompt to test problem framing, not a rule favouring ambitious redesign.
