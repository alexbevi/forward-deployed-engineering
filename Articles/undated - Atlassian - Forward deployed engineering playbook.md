[Landing page](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook) · [Publisher PDF](https://atlassianblog.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/the-atlassian-forward-deployed-engineering-playbook.pdf).

## Summary

Atlassian describes Discover, Build, Adopt, and Value phases, typically around twelve weeks, combining customer self-sufficiency with platform improvement. Its lessons prioritise frequent, painful, measurable work; governance built into architecture; sustained adoption in existing workflows; and economics across context, compute, and displaced effort.

## Perspective and limits
