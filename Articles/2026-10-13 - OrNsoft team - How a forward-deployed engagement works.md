# How a forward-deployed engagement works

[Read the article](https://www.ornsoft.com/blog/how-a-forward-deployed-engagement-works/).

OrNsoft describes four written gates: signed production definition, security clearance, measured acceptance, and customer operation. Diagnosis may end in a written no-go. The definition names scope, metrics, a frozen evaluation set, security, human approvals, and cut-over conditions. Work uses the customer's repository and deployment environment, with parallel operation before cut-over and weekly written updates.

## Evidence and limits

This is a vendor-described process. No completed gate records, comparative outcomes, or demonstrated handover are supplied. The quoted client review does not validate the whole model; the review and linked standards were not inspected.
