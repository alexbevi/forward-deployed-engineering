# How an experienced Staff engineer works

[Read the article](https://newsletter.eng-leadership.com/p/how-an-experienced-staff-engineer).

Ojstersek profiles van Buuren's cross-team technical leadership. A senior engineer leads email workflow automation while she supports its intended wider adoption; a separate SLO initiative involves engineering, product, customer success and operations. She describes mentoring through opportunities matched to colleagues' interests and team needs, review of failure scenarios, and using reviews to share reasoning.

These are reported experiences, without measured mentoring or adoption outcomes. The newsletter republishes a case study from *The Multiplier Mindset*, so those versions share provenance.
