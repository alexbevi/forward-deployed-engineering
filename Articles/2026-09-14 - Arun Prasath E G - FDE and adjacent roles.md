# FDE and adjacent roles

[Read the article](https://apeg.dev/forward-deployed-engineer/vs-solutions-engineer/).

## Summary

Prasath compares timing, incentives, artifacts, and accountability:

| Role | Author's framing |
| --- | --- |
| Solutions engineer | Demonstrates product fit before purchase. |
| Sales engineer | Similar technical sales work, with revenue incentives. |
| Solutions architect | Designs the system, usually handing implementation to others. |
| FDE | Designs, builds, and remains accountable through production and handover. |

He also distinguishes product-roadmap engineering and model engineering from customer-specific integration work. In small engagements, design and implementation can belong to the same person.

## Perspective and limits

This buyer-oriented comparison supports the author's commercial offering. Its title boundaries, compensation assumptions, and responsibility claims are not a universal taxonomy. It acknowledges overlap between solutions and sales engineering. Verify actual scope and incentives before applying the distinctions.
