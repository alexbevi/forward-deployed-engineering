# FDE evolution at HappyRobot

[Read the article](https://www.happyrobot.ai/blog/past-present-and-future-of-the-forward-deployed-engineer-at-happyrobot).

## Summary

Becker describes a shift from defined solution delivery to embedded missions owning discovery through production. Customer proximity revealed wider opportunities. Accumulated integration and operational knowledge reportedly accelerates delivery. He expects better tooling to free time for domain expertise, workflow improvement, evaluation, and product feedback.

## Perspective and limits

This is HappyRobot's internal account and future direction, without comparative outcome measurements. It does not establish a general industry trajectory. The visible September date is an update; publication is recorded as March in structured metadata.
