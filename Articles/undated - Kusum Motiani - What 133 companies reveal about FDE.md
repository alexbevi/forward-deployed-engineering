# What 133 companies reveal about FDE

[Read the GoodFirms report](https://www.goodfirms.co/resources/forward-deployed-engineering-over-it-staff-augmentation).

## Contribution

GoodFirms reports surveying 133 AI and software companies in June–July 2026. It distinguishes FDE through outcome accountability. Respondents describe varied organisational arrangements and predominantly remote integration. Pure outcome pricing accounts for 21.4% of FDE-function responses, qualifying the article's simplified pricing comparison.

## Evidence limits

Question bases vary without reported counts; recruitment, response rate, questionnaire, and raw data are unavailable in the article. Adoption percentages cannot establish market prevalence. Provider testimonials do not demonstrate comparative effectiveness. Blank-sheet redesigns and future expectations are proposals and forecasts. Salary estimates use separate aggregators, not survey responses.

## Vault interpretation and next evidence

Use [[Operating models/Outcome-scoped missions]] as a comparison lens, not a claim that these respondents share HappyRobot's model. The survey does not establish a new coherent engagement model.
