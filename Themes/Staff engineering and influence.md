# Staff engineering and influence

How do senior individual contributors shape technical systems, decisions, collaboration, and engineering capability beyond their own delivery?

## Catalogue

- [[Articles/2020-11-28 - Will Larson - What do Staff engineers actually do|Larson, 2020]]. Shared duties across Staff archetypes: technical direction, sponsorship, engineering decision context, exploration, glue work, and variable coding.
- [[Articles/2020-09-04 - Will Larson - Work on what matters|Larson, 2020]]. Work-selection failure modes and a conditional prioritisation lens based on consequence, room, attention, completion, and distinctive capability.
- [[Articles/2026-08-26 - Anna Selway - Staff engineers shift from solving problems to shaping systems|Selway, 2026]]. Problem selection, system-level intervention, alignment, and impact that persists without the originating engineer.
- [[Articles/2026-08-18 - James Socol - Staff engineers must rebuild team culture|Socol, 2026]]. Deliberate cultural reinforcement when AI enables more independent work.

## Questions for future sources

- How do organisations distinguish Staff scope from strong senior-engineer execution?
- What evidence shows that an engineer changed a system or decision process rather than merely delivered a large project?
- Which forms of influence preserve useful disagreement instead of manufacturing consensus?
- How should Staff engineers choose between removing coordination costs and preserving interdependent learning?
- Which Staff practices transfer to field-facing work, and which depend on stable internal teams or organisational authority?
