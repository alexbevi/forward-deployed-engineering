# Staff engineering and influence

How do senior individual contributors shape technical systems, decisions, collaboration, and engineering capability beyond their own delivery?

## Catalogue

- [Larson, 2020](../Articles/2020-11-28%20-%20Will%20Larson%20-%20What%20do%20Staff%20engineers%20actually%20do.md). Shared duties across Staff archetypes: technical direction, sponsorship, engineering decision context, exploration, glue work, and variable coding.
- [Larson, 2020](../Articles/2020-09-04%20-%20Will%20Larson%20-%20Work%20on%20what%20matters.md). Work-selection failure modes and a conditional prioritisation lens based on consequence, room, attention, completion, and distinctive capability.
- [Selway, 2026](../Articles/2026-08-26%20-%20Anna%20Selway%20-%20Staff%20engineers%20shift%20from%20solving%20problems%20to%20shaping%20systems.md). Problem selection, system-level intervention, alignment, and impact that persists without the originating engineer.
- [Socol, 2026](../Articles/2026-08-18%20-%20James%20Socol%20-%20Staff%20engineers%20must%20rebuild%20team%20culture.md). Deliberate cultural reinforcement when AI enables more independent work.

## Questions for future sources

- How do organisations distinguish Staff scope from strong senior-engineer execution?
- What evidence shows that an engineer changed a system or decision process rather than merely delivered a large project?
- Which forms of influence preserve useful disagreement instead of manufacturing consensus?
- How should Staff engineers choose between removing coordination costs and preserving interdependent learning?
- Which Staff practices transfer to field-facing work, and which depend on stable internal teams or organisational authority?

This theme covers technical leadership by individual contributors, not people-management processes. [Staff Engineer](../Staff%20Engineer.md) holds the evolving synthesis.
