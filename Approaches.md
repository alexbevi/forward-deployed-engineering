# Approaches

Reusable technical methods that may help an FDE solve a problem. Research can inform these even when it says little about the FDE role.

![[Bases/Approaches.base]]

Start with [[Approaches/Deployment verification through fresh replay]] or [[Approaches/Contract-based governed task transfer]]. Approach notes explain a mechanism, fit, limits and transfer assumptions; [[Playbooks]] turns selected approaches into proposed procedures. [[Operating models]] describes engagement responsibility instead. Local results belong in the relevant approach or playbook.
