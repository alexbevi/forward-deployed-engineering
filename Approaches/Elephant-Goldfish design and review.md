# Elephant-Goldfish design and review

## Source-reported mechanism

[Rensin](../Articles/2026-04-27%20-%20Dave%20Rensin%20-%20Elephants%20Goldfish%20and%20software%20engineering.md) describes context-rich drafting followed by fresh-session review. The [plugin architecture](https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model/blob/3d0b34821ce88ad328b0c9bf0e06d7eb5f1cd669/docs/designs/architecture.md) turns this into specification authoring, staged reviews, human resolution and implementation against an approved document. Review covers architectural simplicity, comprehension, design criticism and readiness. This is a technical method, separate from an FDE engagement operating model.

## Conditions and limits

Fresh reviewers must receive the document, relevant source references and explicit standards without the author's conversation. The [integration documentation](https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model/blob/3d0b34821ce88ad328b0c9bf0e06d7eb5f1cd669/docs/codex.md) warns that conversation isolation does not isolate files, memory services or project instructions.

Our interpretation is that this tests whether the written design carries sufficient context. It does not establish independent correctness. Reviewers can share model errors, miss requirements absent from both document and rubric, or converge on a stale specification. Human judgment, source checks and executable acceptance evidence still matter.

## Proposed evaluation

Compare review conditions on the same bounded design with known omissions and acceptance criteria fixed separately. Record reviewer context, model and version, repetitions, valid findings, false positives, accepted repairs and complete human and model cost. A clean review alone is not evidence of a correct implementation.

No local application is recorded.
