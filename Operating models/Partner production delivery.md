# Partner production delivery

## What is established

[2026-10-01 - Karthik Sonti et al - FDE pathways for AWS partners](../Articles/2026-10-01%20-%20Karthik%20Sonti%20et%20al%20-%20FDE%20pathways%20for%20AWS%20partners.md) presents partner readiness through Ground, Orchestrate, and Prove capability pathways. These are complementary skill areas, not fixed job titles or distinct delivery models.

## What remains unknown

This is a partial operating-model description. The announcement does not specify an engagement lifecycle, decision rights, support ownership, or a requirement to change the vendor's product. Credential attainment is not evidence of a particular customer's business outcome.

## Proposed use

Use the capability areas as prompts when reviewing a team's coverage of data and context, dependable workflows, evaluation, and controls. Verify the live programme before making credential decisions. We have not validated its exercises or used it locally.

See [AWS Partner FDE pathways](../Resources/AWS%20Partner%20FDE%20pathways.md) and [Evaluate outcomes and reuse](../Playbooks/Evaluate%20outcomes%20and%20reuse.md). Return to [Operating models](../Operating%20models.md).
