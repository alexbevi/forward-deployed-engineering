# Atlassian co-engineering

## Reported model

[PDF page 6](../Articles/undated%20-%20Atlassian%20-%20Forward%20deployed%20engineering%20playbook.md) describes Discover, Build, Adopt, and Value. The stated outcomes combine customer capability and product improvement, illustrating that [Customer-owned delivery](Customer-owned%20delivery.md) and [Product-oriented FDE](Product-oriented%20FDE.md) can overlap.

## What remains unknown

The playbook does not establish contractual decision rights, maintenance obligations, or how self-sufficiency is assessed in each engagement. Its duration and results are vendor reports, not demonstrated fit for another organisation.

## Proposed comparison questions

Who can approve production and platform changes? Which receiving team can continue the work? What demonstrates sustained use? How are operating costs measured? Record these answers before borrowing the model.

See [Stage agent autonomy](../Playbooks/Stage%20agent%20autonomy.md) and return to [Operating models](../Operating%20models.md).
