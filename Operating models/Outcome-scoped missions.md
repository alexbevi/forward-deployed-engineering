# Outcome-scoped missions

## Mandate and mechanics

[[Articles/2026-03-18 - Carlos Becker - FDE evolution at HappyRobot]] describes focused missions with outcome ownership from discovery through production. Domain knowledge and integration patterns reportedly reduce surprises. It anticipates faster implementation freeing time for evaluation and deeper product learning.

## Decision rights and ownership

The catalogue does not yet establish HappyRobot's detailed decision rights, maintenance agreements, or exit process. Record them as unknown rather than borrowing them from another company's model.

## Fit and failure modes

A candidate for a bounded workflow where impact can be measured and users can provide feedback. Efficiency claims need a baseline. Finding opportunities beyond the mission does not itself authorise extra work.
