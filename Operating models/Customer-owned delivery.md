# Customer-owned delivery

## Mandate and mechanics

[2026-09-14 - Arun Prasath E G - How an FDE engagement works](../Articles/2026-09-14%20-%20Arun%20Prasath%20E%20G%20-%20How%20an%20FDE%20engagement%20works.md) and [2026-09-14 - Arun Prasath E G - FDE engagements for owners](../Articles/2026-09-14%20-%20Arun%20Prasath%20E%20G%20-%20FDE%20engagements%20for%20owners.md) describe written owner decisions, usable increments, and a named handover. [2026-09-29 - Gartner - Vendor FDE abandonment forecast](../Articles/2026-09-29%20-%20Gartner%20-%20Vendor%20FDE%20abandonment%20forecast.md) focuses on independence at transition. These sources align on ownership while differing in their definitions of the FDE role.

## Decision rights and ownership

[OrNsoft](../Articles/2026-10-13%20-%20OrNsoft%20team%20-%20How%20a%20forward-deployed%20engagement%20works.md) adds written phase gates, but supplies no demonstrated customer handover.

Distinguish business choices, engineering choices, approval for live changes, and continuing support. [2026-09-21 - Pablo Bermejo - Turning intelligence into lasting value](../Articles/2026-09-21%20-%20Pablo%20Bermejo%20-%20Turning%20intelligence%20into%20lasting%20value.md) allows understood external dependencies; independence means the receiving organisation can make informed decisions and change the system without one person's memory.

[CTERA](../Articles/2026-10-06%20-%20CTERA%20-%20Forward%20deployed%20engineering%20for%20enterprise%20AI.md) proposes co-building and documenting from discovery, with completion when customer engineers can add the next use case without the vendor. This is a useful candidate demonstration of transfer, but the press release reports an intended model rather than a completed handover.

## Fit and failure modes

A candidate when the customer has an identified problem, practical access, a domain reviewer, and a capable receiving owner. A running prototype and a document alone do not establish independence. Unowned operations or inaccessible knowledge can make the engagement permanent by accident.

## Limits

The APEG pages overlap and represent one commercial practice. Gartner's rates are forecasts, not measured outcomes. This model does not settle whether customer-specific delivery qualifies as FDE under a product-oriented definition.

See [Scope an engagement](../Playbooks/Scope%20an%20engagement.md) and [Prove a handover](../Playbooks/Prove%20a%20handover.md). Return to [Operating models](../Operating%20models.md).
